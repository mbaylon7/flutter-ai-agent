Rules

  • Pills are between user and AI bubbles, not inside them.

  • Done: 🌐 Read 4 pages ▾ · tap to expand.
  • Failed: red border, plain explanation. Doesn't break the chat.
  • Multiple tool calls stack vertically in the order they ran.

Plain words on the pills, never raw function names ("searched the web," not "web.search()").
