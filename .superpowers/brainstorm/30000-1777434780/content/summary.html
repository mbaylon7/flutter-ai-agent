OpenClaw Mobile — UI/UX summary

Decisions locked in this brainstorm. Use this to walk your team through what we settled on.

1 Visual vibe

Dark / Futuristic
Deep navy gradient, glowing blue ring, neon waveform. Premium feel, easier on eyes.

2 Navigation

Voice-first home
Mic ring is the hero. Swipe up → chat, swipe right → sessions. Feels like an assistant, not a chat app.

3 Mic in chat

Mini mic in composer
Tap mic anytime in chat = instant voice. Familiar pattern (WhatsApp).

4 Voice states

Idle · Listening · Thinking · Speaking
Four distinct ring/animation states. Live transcript while listening, karaoke highlight while speaking.

5 Sessions list

Pinned + Recent, with filters
Search bar, filter chips (All/Voice/Text/Pinned), voice 🎙 vs text 💬 indicator. Avatar + ⚙ at the bottom.

6 Onboarding

Sign up · Provision · Ready
Welcome → Email/password → "Setting up your AI…" → Start talking. No URLs, no tokens, ever.

7 Settings

Account-focused, no gateway
Profile card, Account (profile/plan/sign out), Privacy (clear cache, delete account), Help. Voice/personality/appearance hidden until they ship.

8 Tool calls

Collapsed sources pill
Quiet "📎 3 sources ▾" line under each AI bubble. Tap → bottom sheet with the list. No noise.

9 Error / empty states

Plain-words copy
First launch greeting, offline banner, "waking up your assistant," mic-denied with deeplink to settings.

What this design assumes

  • Audience: non-techy end users.
  • Hosting model: each user gets their own OpenClaw, auto-provisioned by your back end.
  • First slice ships: sign-up + chat mode + sessions + settings shell + the existing speech mode (already in the POC).
  • Wake word, advanced voice mode UI, plugins, themes, tones — later slices.

Open dependencies (outside this Flutter project)

  • A back-end provisioning service (creates per-user OpenClaw containers, handles auth, billing).
  • Decisions on plan tiers, container sleep/wake policy, support contact.
  • Final approval from your superiors on the each-user-own-OpenClaw model.
Next: once your superiors confirm the hosting model, we lock the design spec and move to the implementation plan for slice 1.
